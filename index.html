How I work

Ground first, plan second

A project plan is only as good as my understanding of the ground it sits on. So I go and get that understanding myself.

On site

I go where the problem lives

Forward-deployed delivery: I work at client sites, walk the operation and talk to the people doing the job before I commit to scope. Industrial plants, warehouses, OT and IIoT environments included.

In the build

I touch the stack, not just the tickets

I've built RPA flows, AI agents and prototypes myself, and worked hands-on with MCP, vector databases and CI/CD. When I estimate, I know what the work involves.

At the root

I dig until I find the real cause

Root cause analysis is a regular part of how I run delivery. A fix that only treats the symptom comes back in the next sprint.

In the details

I handle the unglamorous parts

On the WMS project I sourced and onboarded the hardware vendors myself. I own SOWs, costing models, SLAs and AMC renewals, so the commercial side matches what's on the ground.
